## Abdulla Al Kafy

**GeoAI researcher and PhD student, Geography and the Environment, The University of Texas at Austin**

I build geospatial AI that measures environmental exposure (urban heat, air quality, land change) and then tests whether the result holds up: when the map unit changes, against ground measurements, and against what people actually experience.

- 60+ peer-reviewed journal articles (21 as first author), 6,980 citations, h-index 45 on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vkBgMkIAAAAJ) (October 2026)
- Field program across all 254 Texas counties: about 1,400 measurement sites, 13 seasonal campaigns, 250,000+ calibrated readings
- Teaching Environmental GIS and Land Use/Land Cover Change Practicum in UT Austin's GeoAI Undergraduate Certificate

### Featured

**[GeoXVIEW](https://github.com/AbdullaKafy/GeoXVIEW)**: scale-adaptive environmental exposure framework for Texas. Python package, open site data, and a [live dashboard](https://abdullakafy.github.io/GeoXVIEW/) across five map units. Shows that 8.2% of sites change exposure class depending on the map unit.

**[publications-code](https://github.com/AbdullaKafy/publications-code)**: every journal article with DOI, citations, and links to companion code as it is released.
